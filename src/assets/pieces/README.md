# Piece art

## Chessnut (standard pieces)

`wK wQ wR wB wN wP` and `bK bQ bR bB bN bP` are the **Chessnut** set by **Alexis Luengas**, taken
unmodified from the [lichess](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/chessnut)
repository, where it is listed as Apache License 2.0. They are licensed under the Apache License,
Version 2.0 ([LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt](LICENSE-Apache-2.0.txt)), not under this project's MIT License.

## Fairy pieces (generated)

The fairy piece icons are generated by [`scripts/make-fairy-pieces.mjs`](../../../scripts/make-fairy-pieces.mjs);
edit that script and re-run it rather than editing the SVGs.

- **Fusion pieces** `T A C Z` (Centaur, Archbishop, Chancellor, Amazon) combine two Chessnut pieces
  (the partner piece behind a knight). They are modified versions of the Chessnut art and stay under
  the Apache License 2.0; the modification is that composition.
- **Other fairy pieces** `E F W M L G X` (Berolina pawn, Ferz, Wazir, Man, Camel, Grasshopper,
  Cannon) are original drawings for this project, under its MIT License.
