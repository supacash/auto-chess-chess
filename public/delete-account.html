In the game

  1. Open the game and tap Profile on the menu.
  2. Under your Google account, tap Delete account, then Tap again to delete.
  3. If Google asks you to sign in again first, do so: the deletion finishes straight after.

This deletes your account and the progress saved to it (records, achievements, best scores, dismissed tips, your player name and the game in progress) from our servers straight away. The copy on your device stays until you clear the game's storage (your browser's site data, or the app's storage in Android settings).
