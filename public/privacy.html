Privacy policy

Draft

Pawned Up, the chess auto battler, on the web and in the Android app. Last updated: [?]. Developer: [? name]. Contact: [? email].

The short version

  • No account, no email address and no real name needed to play.
  • Single-player games, records and settings stay on your device.
  • Playing online sends your army, results and a randomly generated player name (like "Golden Knight 19") to Google Firebase, under an anonymous ID.
  • The Android app can show a rewarded ad when you choose to watch one; Google's ad service then collects device data as described below. The web version has no ads.
  • The game has no analytics or tracking of its own.

Stored on your device

The game keeps these in your browser's or app's local storage. They never leave your device unless you play online (see the next section).

  • The game in progress, including the opponent drafted for the next round, and the daily challenge in progress.
  • Your records and achievements (daily challenge results and streak included), best scores, and your last battle (for Review).
  • The online match you're in, so you can rejoin it (its room code and round).
  • Your generated player name.
  • Your settings: sound on or off, effects volume, battle speed, reduced motion, and which tips you've dismissed.

Clearing the site's data in your browser, or the app's storage in Android settings, deletes all of it. Settings → Show again clears just the dismissed tips.

Online matches

Multiplayer uses Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Nothing is sent to Firebase until you create, join or rejoin an online game (Quick play, Create room, Join room or Rejoin match). Play vs bots stays on your device.

  • Anonymous sign-in. The game signs in to Firebase anonymously: Firebase gives your device a random user ID. No name, email or phone number is asked for. Firebase keeps the sign-in on your device.
  • Rooms. A room stores its code, its rules and game version, and for each player: the user ID, generated player name, the army placed each round, perks picked, readiness, and battle results. Anyone signed in to the game can read a room (to join it by code); a player's army stays hidden until that round's shop has closed.
  • Clock. To agree on round timers, the game writes the server's time to a small record under your user ID.
  • Firebase's servers, like any web service, receive your device's IP address and basic connection details when the game talks to them. Google processes this data under its data processing terms. The database is hosted in the United States (Firestore location nam5).
  • How long room data is kept: [?] At the moment, a room is deleted only when its host leaves it empty in the lobby; finished rooms are not deleted.

Ads in the Android app

The Android app can show rewarded ads, only when you choose to watch one (for example, for an extra life). They're served by Google AdMob. The web version shows no ads.

  • The Google Mobile Ads SDK collects data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, and information about the device and the ads you see, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. See how Google uses this data.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before ads load.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
